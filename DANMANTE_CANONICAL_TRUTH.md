Expand access to affordable, secure, and professionally delivered healthcare support through a global, jurisdiction-aware digital platform — particularly for vulnerable populations and communities facing healthcare-access shortages.

## Clinical Principle
Danmante does not replace emergency services, licensed clinical judgment, or the healthcare regulations of any jurisdiction in which it operates.

## AI Principle
AI is a clinical and administrative **support** layer. AI does not diagnose, prescribe, alter prescriptions, override a licensed professional, or determine that an emergency can be safely ignored. Every AI-assisted clinical output is labeled as AI-assisted and subject to professional review.

## Data Principle
Medical records, diagnoses, consultation notes, and prescriptions are never stored on a blockchain. Blockchain, where used, is a payment/transaction rail only.

## Global Principle
Every clinical, prescribing, or pharmacy workflow must pass through the Jurisdiction & Clinical Rules Engine. If a jurisdiction's rules are not established, the workflow fails closed (blocked), never open.
