# AI Safety Policy

## Purpose

This repository uses AI only as an assistive layer for non-authoritative workflow support, not as a substitute for licensed clinical judgment or jurisdictional authorization.

## Allowed uses

- Translation and localization support
- Intake organization
- Administrative summarization
- Scheduling assistance
- Accessibility and navigation support
- General educational content that does not replace clinical judgment

## Disallowed uses

- Diagnosis
- Prescribing
- Medication authorization
- Emergency triage decisions
- Approval of nurse or pharmacy credentialing
- Override of jurisdiction rules
- Silent modification of clinical records

## Requirement

Any AI-assisted output must be clearly labeled, attributable, reviewable, and subject to professional oversight where required.
