# Privacy Policy

## Current status

Danmante is an engineering foundation and does not yet operationalize a full production privacy program. The project is designed to support applicable privacy and security requirements, but no jurisdiction-specific legal or privacy review has been completed.

## Data handling principles

- classify personal and health-related data before storage or processing
- keep patient, clinician, pharmacy, and admin data separated by role and access control
- avoid placing clinical or identity data on public blockchain rails
- require explicit consent or legal basis for processing when used in a deployment
- maintain tamper-evident audit records for privacy-sensitive actions

## Data categories

The repository recognizes that personal and health data may include:

- account and identity data
- professional credentials and license metadata
- appointment and scheduling data
- clinical notes and encounter metadata
- pharmacy and medication workflow records
- payment and account metadata

## Retention and deletion

The repository documents the need for retention rules, deletion policies, and legal-hold handling. These controls are not yet fully implemented in a production workflow.

## External review

Any real-world deployment must receive privacy and data-protection review for the applicable jurisdiction before patient or professional use.

## Important note

This policy is not a claim of HIPAA, GDPR, or other formal compliance.
